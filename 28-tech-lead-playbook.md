# Chapter 28 - Tech Lead Playbook

## Learning Objectives

After completing this chapter, you will:

- Understand the responsibilities of a Tech Lead
- Lead architecture and technical decisions
- Conduct effective design and code reviews
- Manage technical debt and production incidents
- Mentor engineers and grow teams
- Plan roadmaps and align stakeholders
- Track engineering effectiveness using metrics

---

# Part I. What Is a Tech Lead

## Responsibilities

- Technical Leadership
- Architecture Ownership
- Team Enablement
- Delivery Ownership
- Risk Management
- Stakeholder Communication

## Senior Engineer vs Tech Lead

Senior Engineer:
- Focuses on individual technical impact

Tech Lead:
- Multiplies team impact
- Guides technical direction
- Balances delivery and architecture

---

# Part II. Technical Leadership

## Lead by Example

Good Tech Leads:
- Coach instead of command
- Review instead of criticize
- Support instead of micromanage

## Decision Making

Framework:

```text
Business Value
    +
Technical Quality
    +
Delivery Speed
```
